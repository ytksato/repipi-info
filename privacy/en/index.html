Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy describes how the Repipi iPhone app and this public website handle data.

1. Basic Policy

Repipi processes, organizes, and stores recipes on your device. Repipi has no feature that automatically uploads saved recipes, photos, or cooking records to the developer’s server. URL fetching, Apple’s StoreKit, opening external links, and emails you choose to send are separate flows described below. iOS backup behavior depends on your device settings.

2. Processing That Stays On Your Device

Extracting recipes from screenshots (image recognition and text structuring), parsing ingredients and amounts, suggesting substitutions, search, tagging, and cooking mode all run using on-device capabilities such as Apple Intelligence. These image-recognition and text-processing operations run on your device. OS activities such as obtaining photos, preparing model availability, and backing up data depend on your device settings and state.

When importing screenshots, the app may request photo-library access to read capture dates for the selected images. If a capture date cannot be read, it checks the image's own metadata; without a date, the image becomes an import candidate with no capture time. It does not modify or delete original images in your photo library.

3. Communication for Recipe URL Fetching

This section describes recipe URL fetching. When you import from a URL you entered or shared, the app connects to retrieve that page. StoreKit (Section 5), opening external links, website hosting (Section 8), and contact emails (Section 7) are separate flows.

  • This fetching feature contacts the permitted destination and redirect destinations.
  • Fetching a web page technically requires sending some information to that site — the URL, your IP address, the User-Agent, and Accept-Language, among other standard HTTP request details.
  • This fetching feature uses encrypted HTTPS only and does not send cookies or login credentials.
  • This fetching feature does not upload saved recipe text, photos, or cooking records as a request body. The URL or query itself may contain information. This fetching configuration does not control communication or cookies when you open external links in a browser.
  • When you import a URL through the iOS share sheet, the fetch begins automatically as soon as Repipi opens — potentially before you've seen the in-app URL screen or Settings. The share sheet itself shows a brief notice before closing on its own after about a second, which may not be enough time to read it in full. Please be aware of this in advance.

4. App Usage Analytics and Advertising Tracking

Repipi has no usage-analytics or advertising-tracking feature. Section 8 describes information handled as part of website hosting.

5. Purchases

Repipi uses Apple’s StoreKit to obtain product information, check purchase status, and make or restore the one-time purchase. These operations use Apple’s services and may involve communication with Apple depending on device state. Repipi and its developer do not directly handle payment details such as card numbers.

6. Deleting Your Data

Use Delete All Data in Settings to request deletion of recipes, cooking records, received images and URLs, and other data stored in Repipi. This applies to data and image copies inside Repipi; it does not delete original photos in your photo library. The app reports a failed database deletion or files left behind after a database deletion. This deletion operation applies to data and image copies inside Repipi. It does not delete contact emails sent separately, logs held by third-party services, or OS backups.

7. Contact

For questions about using Repipi, bugs, privacy, or concerns from rights holders about recipe imports, contact contact@dplabo.com.

Contact details are also available on the support page. Text and attachments you choose to include in an email are sent to the contact address through your email service. Recipes and photos stored in the app are not automatically attached or sent.

8. About This Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Viewing its pages involves communication with GitHub. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

See GitHub Pages’ data-collection explanation for details.

This site’s HTML/CSS does not embed external fonts, advertising, analytics or session-replay scripts, or submission forms.
